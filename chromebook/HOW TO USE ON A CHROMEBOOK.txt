ZULU 1 PRO ON A CHROMEBOOK
Two tutors: Master Shakai ("Zulu 1 Tutor") and Master Bubba ("Zulu 1 Bubba").
Each keeps its own stars and words.

BEST WAY: INSTALL FROM THE WEBSITE (puppy icon in the launcher, own window, always up to date)
1. Open Chrome and go to:
     Master Shakai: https://bajanbob.github.io/Zulu1/
     Master Bubba:  https://bajanbob.github.io/Zulu1/bubba/
2. Click the Install icon at the right of the address bar (a small screen with a down arrow),
   or open the Chrome menu (three dots) > Cast, save and share > Install page as app.
3. Click Install. The tutor now opens from the launcher like any other app.

OFFLINE WAY: USE THE FILES IN THIS ZIP (no internet needed, except for Zulu 1 Pro)
1. Open the Files app, double-click this zip, and copy the "Zulu 1 Pro - Chromebook" folder
   into My files (for example into Downloads).
2. Open the "Master Shakai" or "Master Bubba" folder and double-click the .html file.
   It opens in Chrome.
3. Press Ctrl + D to bookmark it so it is easy to find next time.
Always open the same copy of the file, because stars and words are saved for that copy.

VOICE
Chromebooks have natural Google UK English voices. In the tutor, open Beryl > Voice samples
to hear them and choose one.

ZULU 1 PRO
Open Beryl > Zulu 1 Pro, choose "My own Claude API key", paste the key, Save, then Test connection.
